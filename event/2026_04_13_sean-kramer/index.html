Drawing from his frontline experience covering the NCAA, MLB, and the CPBL, Sean taught us how to break free from rigid templates and use engaging journalistic logic to ’tell a story.’ I immediately applied these skills to my latest short video, summarizing my journey with English as a Medium of Instruction (EMI) over the past few years.

Action Over Inspiration: My Practical Output from the Workshop!

這次參加運休學院辦的 Sean Kramer 英語寫作與新聞工作坊，原本以為會是那種很正經、偏學術的寫作課，結果完全出乎意料！Sean 用他以前跑 NCAA、MLB，甚至在疫情期間來台灣採訪中職的實戰經驗，把「如何說好一個故事 (Storytelling)」這件事講得超生動。 Attending the English Writing and Journalism Workshop hosted by Sean Kramer was totally beyond my expectations! I initially thought it would be another formal, academic writing class. Instead, Sean used his rich frontline experience covering the NCAA, MLB, and even the CPBL during the pandemic to vividly demonstrate the art of “storytelling.”

他教我們怎麼抓重點、怎麼鋪陳，把新聞從業人員那套精準又有吸引力的敘事邏輯，直接套用到我們平常的英文表達上。這對我來說超級受用，因為不管是做學術簡報，還是經營社群，我們其實都在嘗試把複雜的資訊「翻譯」成大家想聽的故事。 He taught us how to nail the core message and structure the narrative, applying the precise and engaging logic of journalism directly to our daily English expression. This was incredibly helpful for me, because whether giving academic presentations or managing social media, we are essentially trying to “translate” complex information into stories people actually want to hear.

從輸入到輸出：我的第一支全英文短影音

From Input to Output: My First All-English Short Video

既然課程裡有提到短影音競賽的資訊，我覺得最好的學習就是直接動手做。我馬上把工作坊學到的技巧拿來實踐，重新梳理了自己這四年來在師大參與 EMI (English as a Medium of Instruction) 的歷程，還有年初去亞利桑那州立大學 (ASU) 受訓的經驗，拍成了一支短影音！ Since the workshop mentioned the upcoming short-video contest, I figured the best way to learn is by doing. I immediately put the techniques from the workshop into practice, reorganized my 4-year EMI journey at NTNU and my recent training experience at Arizona State University (ASU) earlier this year, and shot a short video!

沒有用生硬的稿子，而是嘗試用更自然、更有連結感的節奏來分享。說到做到，這是我剛熱騰騰產出的成品，歡迎大家去看看： No stiff scripts—just a natural and connective rhythm to share my thoughts. As promised, here is the fresh output. Feel free to check it out:

未來應用：讓運動科學走出國際

Future Applications: Taking Sports Science Global

這次工作坊給我最大的啟發是，英文不只是一個工具，它是我們與全球社群建立連結的橋樑。 The biggest takeaway from this workshop is that English is not just a tool; it’s a bridge to connect with the global community.

未來，我會把這套新聞敘事技巧更多地應用在 Coach Steve Performance 的頻道經營上。不管是在分享阻力訓練、血流限制訓練 (BFR) 的最新運動科學文獻，還是在未來的全英文授課 (EMI) 中，我都希望能用更道地、更有感染力的方式，把台灣的運科知識跟全世界的受眾分享。 Moving forward, I will apply these journalistic storytelling skills more extensively to my Coach Steve Performance channel. Whether I’m breaking down the latest sports science literature on resistance training and Blood Flow Restriction (BFR) or conducting future EMI courses, I hope to share Taiwan’s sports science insights with a global audience in a more authentic and impactful way.

再次感謝 Sean Kramer 帶來的精彩內容與滿滿啟發！也很期待 5 月 25 日的第二場工作坊，再來去挖更多寶！ Thanks again to Sean Kramer for the fantastic content and endless inspiration! I’m really looking forward to the second session on May 25th to dig up more gems!
